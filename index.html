Story

Twenty seats, below Perilla Fare

“NORIKO handroll bar is a new sushi restaurant concept specializing in handrolls which are prepared immediately in front of you. Each unique handroll is packed with flavor and carefully crafted with high quality ingredients in this intimate underground space” (OpenTable). Chefs Billy Lim and Rhan Whang run the counter.

  • ✓

    Made to order, in front of you — handrolls prepared immediately at the counter — never sitting, never soggy.

  • ✓

    The Doshi Doshi — bluefin tuna toro, uni and caviar — the bite diners write home about.

  • ✓

    Homakase to go — NORIKO's to-go box with compartments for sushi, sashimi, nori, sauces and chopsticks.
